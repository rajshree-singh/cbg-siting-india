Source data in `data/raw/` and `data/external/` remain under the terms of their providers:
- GOBARdhan CBG portal, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India (https://gobardhan.gov.in), accessed 22 October 2026.
- District-wise crop area, production and yield: UPAG / Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, via India Data Portal.
- 20th Livestock Census 2019 (district-wise): Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying, via data.gov.in, Government Open Data License – India.
- Maharashtra district livestock figures: Animal Husbandry Department, Government of Maharashtra, 20th Livestock Census 2019 report, Table 3.11.
- Census-2011 district boundaries: datameet/maps, CC BY 2.5 India.
